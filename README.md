# Xinyi · Translations

Personal homepage — three experiments in translation, each presented as a small toy you can play with:

- **01 Unfold** — a flat image folds open into a room you can look around · [live](https://whereisxinyi.github.io/resonance-exhibition/)
- **02 Light on Light** — tap the paper and a small gift draws itself · [live](https://light-on-light.vercel.app/)
- **03 The Energy Exchange** — tap and a pulse of energy trades sides · [live](https://me-pi-jade.vercel.app/)

Static site, no framework: `index.html` + `tokens.css` + `styles.css` + `main.js`.

**Live: <https://whereisxinyi.github.io/>**
